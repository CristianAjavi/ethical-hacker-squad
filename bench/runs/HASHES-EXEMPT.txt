# Rounds whose shipped reports carry no REPORTS.sha256

`scripts/gates/lib/scored_matches_shipped.py` requires every round that ships reports to hash them,
so a reader can check that the numbers on the page were computed from these exact bytes. The rounds
below ran BEFORE that check existed and have no hash file.

They are not backfilled on purpose. Hashing them today would prove that nobody edits them from now
on; it would prove nothing about whether they match what was scored, and a reader seeing a hash file
would reasonably assume the stronger claim. A missing hash that is written down is worth more than a
present hash that means less than it looks.

So the exemption is a list, not a silence. The check fails if a round without hashes is absent here,
and fails if a round listed here has acquired them - a stale exemption is the same defect as a
missing one. The list can only shrink by re-running a round under the check, never by editing it.

Counted on 2026-08-28: 22 rounds, 173 reports exempt, against 12 rounds and 168 reports covered.
