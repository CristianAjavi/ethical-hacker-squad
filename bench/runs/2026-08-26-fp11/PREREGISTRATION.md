# Pre-registration — one decoy per run, one named shape, one new rule

Written and pushed **before any arm runs and before this file's author has seen the corpus**.

## The hypothesis is now narrow enough to be wrong cleanly

Seven rounds have met the recall half three times and the conjunction never. The seventh
narrowed the remaining gap to a single number: **one decoy per run** — 3.00 against 2.00.

Diffing them by identity, **exactly two decoys were reported by this project and ruled out by
`mantis`, and both were the same shape**: a wildcard CORS header set only on `/healthz`, and an
auditor role whose scope is joined to the caller's org three files away. `narrowed-sibling` —
a construct as wide as it reads, applying to far less than it appears to.

`FP-11` was added for that and nothing else. **Whether it closes the gap is what this round
measures.**

## The way this round could cheat, and what was done about it

Commissioning a corpus heavy in `narrowed-sibling` decoys would be writing the exam for the
answer. The brief requires the six decoy shapes **evenly spread, none above 7**, and the key
records `shape` per decoy so the distribution is checkable rather than promised. If `FP-11`
only helps under a distribution tuned for it, it has not helped.

## Band, unchanged for the eighth time

Supported iff recall exceeds `mantis`'s by **≥ 5 points** *and* the decoy rate is **not higher**.
Refuted if `mantis`'s recall is greater or equal. Inconclusive if ahead by less than 5.

Failed seven times. Lowered once from 15, with the lowering declared on the page. Never raised.

## Registered, with its own band

> **The decoy rate falls to at or below `mantis`'s**, and recall stays within **3 points** of
> the 78.3% the same procedures reached without `FP-11`.

- **Decoys fall, recall holds** — the rule does what it was added for, on a distribution not
  built for it.
- **Decoys fall and recall drops more than 3 points** — it buys precision by ruling out real
  findings. A trade, and the outcome this round is most likely to be tempted to misreport.
- **Decoys do not fall** — `FP-11` is another sentence in a file. It was derived from two cases
  and it did not generalise, which is the same failure the four tools had, one level up.

## Registered secondary, without a band

**Decoys reported per shape, per arm.** If this project's surplus is concentrated in
`narrowed-sibling` before the rule and spread evenly after, the rule moved what it aimed at. If
the surplus was never concentrated there, the seventh round's two-decoy diff was a coincidence
of small numbers and the whole hypothesis rested on n=2 — which is worth knowing and is not
something the primary can tell.
