# Pre-registration — a field where a rule failed

Written and pushed **before any arm runs and before this file's author has seen the corpus**.

## The hypothesis, in one line

Eight rounds produced one generalisation: **a required field moves a number and a rule does
not.** The triage contract — a field the gate rejects an artifact without — took the decoy rate
from 9.75 to 6.00. `VER-09` fired **zero times** across four measured runs. `FP-11`, cited in
the file every role reads, given an eval case and a sealed key row, left the shape it targets at
**1.75 false positives per run against a competitor's 0.25.**

So the same question is now a required field. Every finding must carry `scope`: `applies_to`
listing every `path:line` where the construct actually takes effect, `established_by` naming how
that list was arrived at, `narrowed_by` when a condition narrows it.

## Registered prediction, with its band

> **`narrowed-sibling` decoys reported fall to at or below `mantis`'s rate on that shape**, and
> total recall stays within **3 points** of the 74.3% the same procedures reached without the
> field.

- **The shape falls and recall holds** — a field does what a rule could not, and the
  generalisation earned across eight rounds holds on a ninth.
- **The shape falls and recall drops more than 3 points** — the field buys precision by
  suppressing real findings. A trade, reported as a trade.
- **The shape does not fall** — *the field is a rule with more ceremony.* That would refute the
  one generalisation this ledger has, and it is the outcome most worth catching, because every
  intervention since would need re-reading in its light.

## Band, unchanged for the ninth time

Supported iff recall exceeds `mantis`'s by **≥ 5 points** *and* the decoy rate is **not higher**.
Refuted if `mantis`'s recall is greater or equal. Inconclusive if ahead by less than 5.

Failed eight times. Lowered once from 15, with the lowering declared on the page. Never raised.

## The corpus, and the cheat this round has to avoid

The brief again requires the six decoy shapes **evenly spread, none above 7**, with
`narrowed-sibling` explicitly told not to be over-weighted, and the key records `shape` per
decoy so the distribution is checkable. A corpus thin in the shape under test would let the
number fall for the wrong reason.

## Registered secondary, without a band

- **Decoys per shape, per arm** — the same cut that made the eighth round legible. It is what
  distinguishes *the field worked* from *this corpus happened to be easier on that shape*.
- **How many findings carry a `scope` with a non-trivial `established_by`**, and whether
  `narrowed_by` is ever populated. A field satisfied by writing the same sentence everywhere is
  a form filled in, which is exactly what this round is testing against.

## Scoring and multiplicity

arm with no per-arm branch; floor of 3 valid runs; four runs per arm; both arms under the
artifact contract. One round, then this corpus is retired.
